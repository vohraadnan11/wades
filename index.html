How We Build

From Wire To Water

Every trap that leaves our shop in Glenwood goes through the same five steps it always has — no shortcuts, no outsourcing.

01

Wire Selection

We start with heavy-gauge galvanized wire, chosen to stand up to salt water and rough handling.

02

Forming

Wire is bent and shaped into the trap frame on jigs we've built and refined in-house.

03

Welding

Every joint is welded by hand for a frame that holds together under load, trip after trip.

04

Coating

Frames get a protective coating to fight corrosion through years spent on the bottom.

05

Assembly & Inspection

Funnels, runners, and hardware go on, and every trap is checked before it leaves the shop.
